### Reflection
Throughout the MAE 3780 robot competition project, my contributions spanned mechanical design, electrical hardware integration, and documentation. During Milestone 1 (design pitch), I collaborated with my teammates Alexander Barry and Nicole Kim to develop the initial PowerPoint presentation and concept for a sweeping-arm robot. For Milestone 2 (mobility), I mounted the wheel servos and wired the servo motors to the breadboard and Arduino Uno, establishing the hardware foundation that allowed Alexander's mobility code to drive the robot. In Milestone 3 (color sensing and border detection), I designed and installed the color sensor mount, then handled the full wiring of the color sensor circuit so that the sensor could feed clean signals into the detection code. For Milestone 4 (cube clearing), I designed and 3D-printed the servo arm mounts and developed the first iteration of the sweeping arm. On the Final Report, I produced the circuit schematic, all CAD files and screenshots, and the program flowchart. Working on this project taught me the value of leveraging each teammate's strengths, staying open to design iteration, and clearly defining individual roles in a competitive setting where iteration time is limited. Our team's strong communication and adherence to our team contract enabled us to go undefeated through the entire first round of the competition.
